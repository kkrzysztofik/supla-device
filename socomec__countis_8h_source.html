59// Sum of per-line energy vs the 50946 totals. The 2026-10-29 probe showed that
60// per-line active energy tracks the system totals to ~0.015 %, while the
61// per-direction reactive counters do not reproduce the system reactive totals
62// (the meter defines them differently). So only active energy is cross-checked,
63// with a relative tolerance that absorbs the installation-history offset; the
64// floor covers a nearly empty counter. See
65// docs/integrations/socomec-countis-probe-2026-10-29.md.
66constexpr uint64_t kCrossCheckFloor = 3000; // 30 Wh, in 0.00001 kWh
67constexpr uint64_t kCrossCheckDivisor = 100; // 1 % of the active totals
